The fixed speeds choose their own sample rate: 100 kS/s for 9600, 250 kS/s for 19200. The buffer is left untouched when the tester is left; only AUTO held on the Buffer row in Settings empties it. The calibration output sits at 0 V, except in TTL Sender, where it sits at 3.3 V (an idle line). On a laptop, the export is received with tools\ttl_receive.ps1 -Port COMx (USB-TTL adapter: RX to the calibration output, ground to ground); the CRC the script prints must match the one on the instrument's screen.
